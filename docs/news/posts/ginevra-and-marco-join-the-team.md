# Ginevra and Marco join the team!

Two new PhD students just joined the group! Ginevra Lautizi, who just graduated from the University of Trieste, and Marco Margini, from the University of Padova. Ginevra will be working on directional dark-field imaging and tomography, Marco on incoherent and coherent scattering in the context of speckle-based imaging and ptychography.
